Coming from Teletype

OType borrows Teletype's prefix notation, its modifiers before : and many of its words. The main differences:

  • no limit of six lines: blocks by indentation, named functions with arguments, instead of scripts 1 to 8;
  • no ;: an indented block replaces several commands on a line;
  • no W (while): every loop is bounded before the run;
  • operations act on a context of tracks and steps;
  • a b c d are local, x y z t global and kept between presses;
  • scripts are written on a computer, since the Octatrack has no keyboard port.
